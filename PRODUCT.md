# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Records, security, and operational teams in regulated organisations use
BlakFile to organise, locate, share, and govern sensitive business records.
They work in focused, high-accountability sessions and need familiar file
management patterns with immediate visibility into security state.

## Product Purpose

BlakFile is a secure, self-hostable file workspace for organisations that need
enterprise-grade sharing, tenancy controls, auditability, and Australian data
sovereignty. Success means users can complete everyday file work as naturally
as they can in Box, Google Drive, or OneDrive while understanding what protects
their content.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, capable, and custodial. The product expresses the visual language of
the BlakFile logo through a restrained black, ochre, earth-red, and warm-ivory
palette, with security and care communicated through purposeful detail rather
than decoration.

## Anti-references

Do not make the product look like a generic AI-generated dashboard, a glossy
consumer app, or an abstract landing page. Avoid glass effects, gratuitous
gradients, novelty navigation, and ornamental patterning that competes with
file-management tasks. Do not imitate Box, Google Drive, or OneDrive; use the
familiarity of their enterprise file-workspace conventions.

## Design Principles

1. Familiar file work comes first: navigation, folders, tables, and actions
   behave as an experienced enterprise user expects.
2. Show governance in context: security state is clear at the file and folder
   level without obstructing everyday work.
3. Carry the mark with restraint: use the logo palette and material warmth to
   distinguish BlakFile without turning product surfaces into illustrations.
4. Keep important information close: users should not hunt for ownership,
   classification, location, or recent activity.
5. Earn trust through clarity: dense, accessible layouts and direct language
   make sensitive work feel controlled.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve strong text and state contrast, support keyboard
navigation with visible focus treatment, use semantic controls and labels, and
respect reduced-motion preferences.
